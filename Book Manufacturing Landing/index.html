The right timber makes the paper

Paper is spun from wood-cellulose fibre. Fibre length is the key: long softwood fibres add strength, short hardwood fibres add smoothness. We blend both for the right feel and durability.

Softwood · long fibre

Southern Pine

Fibre 3–5 mm · high strength

The workhorse reinforcement pulp. Adds tear and fold strength to kraft, packaging, and book covers.

Softwood · long fibre

Spruce

Pale · low resin

Clean, bright mechanical pulp for high-quality printing paper and text stocks.

Hardwood · short fibre

Eucalyptus

Fibre ~1 mm · high yield

The global favourite for fine paper — smooth, bright, opaque text and writing stocks.

Hardwood · short fibre

Birch

Smooth surface

Nordic staple for premium uncoated book papers with a soft, even printing surface.

Hardwood · short fibre

Aspen / Poplar

Bulky · lightweight

Soft and high-bulk — ideal for economical novels, catalogs, and lightweight editions.

Blend · balanced

Our House Blend

~75% eucalyptus + 25% pine

Short-fibre smoothness for crisp print, long-fibre strength for pages that survive re-reading.
